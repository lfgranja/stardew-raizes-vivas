## Research Notes

- Decompiled `Crop.harvest` (1.6.15) confirms extra-harvest items are created as fresh `Object` instances with default `Quality = 0` (normal); they do not inherit the main item's rolled quality and there is no per-extra re-roll (research-c2 §1.3).
- Vanilla harvest blocks the main item on full inventory (red message, crop stays) but always spawns extra-harvest items as ground debris (research-c1).
- The "compose, don't replace" decision is a first-class requirement (FR-003) and is the primary consistency anchor (research-c2 §4).
- Quality fertilizer (num2 1/2/3) interaction is unchanged: mod composes (additive on `p`), never replaces.
- Special cases (forage, fiber, Qi Fruit, fruit trees, giant crops, tea bushes, ground forage) do not roll quality; requirements should state inclusion/exclusion (Gap CHK009).
