## Research Notes

- GAG (gameaccessibilityguidelines.com): color-blind friendly (never color alone), flicker/photosensitivity safety, high contrast (configurable), signposting, eye-line placement, consistent responses, immediate activation (flow/INFORM).
- XAG v3.2: 101 text sizes/scaling/fonts (minimum 18 pt? configurable), 102 contrast ratios (4.5:1 normal, 3:1 large, 7:1 enhanced), 103 non-chromatic cues / color config.
- W3C WCAG: 1.4.1 Use of Color, 1.4.3 Contrast, 1.4.11 Non-text Contrast, 2.2.1 Timing Adjustable, 4.1.3 Status Messages; G111 (color + pattern).
- Transient feedback patterns: avoid toast anti-patterns (Primer); layered notification; immunity to degradation via event-driven render (not per-frame tile pass).
- Performance budgets: 16.67 ms/frame (60 FPS), 1000-tile degradation threshold with 980 hysteresis; feedback must not trigger degradation path.
- Color-blind safety: `PatternAlphaPinned`, `PatternMinOpacity` 0.7, complementary white/black tones (existing visualization domain).
- Photosensitivity: avoid strobing; safe flash durations (existing hoe feedback 300 ms flash + 1000 ms text).
- Research artifact: `/tmp/opencode/research-014/chk-accessibility.md` (214 lines, 60-item draft checklist CHK-A01–A60).
