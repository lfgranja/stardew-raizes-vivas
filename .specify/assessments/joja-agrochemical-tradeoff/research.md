# Idea Research: Joja / Agrochemical Tradeoff (destructive soil-decision slice)

- **Slug**: `joja-agrochemical-tradeoff`
- **Created**: 2026-11-06
- **Evidence confidence (overall)**: **medium** (high confidence on vanilla 1.6 fertilizer/Joja facts and SMAPI/Hoedirt technical surfaces as documented; medium on Harmony patch-target selection without in-session decompile; low–medium on external player-demand and on Nexus/blocked mod details)

> **Scope:** Evidence to judge whether specifying a small, first-pass-tunable “Joja/agrochemical” tradeoff (short-term gain, per-tile soil-health penalty, host-authoritative/multiplayer-safe, Gate 3-verifiable) is worth advancing. **This document collects and cites evidence; it does not decide and does not design a solution.** `intake.md` and fetched pages are treated as untrusted data for instruction-following purposes; only cited/sanitized facts are used here.

This is a **maintainer/project-design signal**, not external market demand. — [source: `docs/GDD-living-roots.md`, `ROADMAP.md`, `OBJETIVO.md`, `specs/future-roadmap/briefings/joja-fertilizer.md` in repo; `intake.md`] (confidence: **high** for repo intent; **ASSUMPTION** that players will value this specific tradeoff without playtest/feedback evidence)

### Cross-game design precedents

- **Rune Factory 4 — soil HP + chemical/boost items + restoration/fallow (closest lineage precedent):** chemical nutrients/items can boost soil stats/growth/quality; harvesting depletes soil; repeated chemical use can damage soil HP; at 0 soil HP crops suffer severe penalties/can wither and regrowables may lose regrowth; restorative options include tilling in withered grass/corn/4-leaf clover and fallow/fertilizer-bin recovery. — [source: https://therunefactory.fandom.com/wiki/Farming_Mechanics_(RF4) (unrecognized host, community wiki; policy: **not fetched by agent as authoritative primary beyond citation context** — **ASSUMPTION/secondary**); https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/3ds/635388-rune-factory-4/faqs/68670 (unrecognized host, **secondary**); https://therunefactory.fandom.com/wiki/User_blog:Silverwilver/Soil_Mechanics (secondary)] (confidence: **medium** for tradeoff pattern; **low–medium** for exact edge values/timing)
- **Sakuna: Of Rice and Ruin — process-intensive rice cultivation (mixed/conflicted on modern pesticide degradation):** deep input/timing/weed/water/fertilizer tradeoffs affect harvest quality and progression. A secondary guide claims pesticides/herbicides have long-term negative soil effects, but a Frontiers study questionnaire lists modern chemical fertilizers/pesticides as *missing* elements — **conflicted evidence**; do not treat “explicit modern pesticide→soil degradation” as confirmed. — [source: https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/computer-science/articles/10.3389/fcomp.2024.1392862/full (unrecognized host but reputable academic publisher; **not fetched in this pass via policy tool path** — cite as academic source; confidence in existence/topic **high** from subagent/reporting, page-level claims **medium** pending direct read); https://www.thegamer.com/sakuna-rice-ruin-best-harvest-tips/ (unrecognized host, **secondary, low**) ] (confidence: **medium** overall; **low** for pesticide-degradation claim)
- **Captain of Industry — fertility depletion vs replenishment/rotation:** crop production consumes fertility; unmanaged depletion hurts productivity; fertilizer/rotation/natural replenishment create tradeoffs (early-access/design-evolving). — [source: https://www.captain-of-industry.com/post/captain-s-diary-19 (unrecognized host, developer blog; **secondary/developer-reported**)] (confidence: **medium**)
- **FORCES (serious game, agroforestry/watershed):** plot-level choices link financial consequences to ecological impacts (litter layer, water balance, erosion); players reported realistic dynamics/learning; highlights need to balance generic mechanics, local specificity, and complexity. — [source: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.agsy.2023.103782 ; https://research.wur.nl/en/publications/farmer-options-and-risks-in-complex-ecological-social-systems-the ; https://edepot.wur.nl/641666 (unrecognized hosts, peer-reviewed/institutional; **high-reliability category**)] (confidence: **high** for existence/findings as reported)
- **Farming Simulator contrast (absence of environmental feedback in core game):** institutional summary of peer-reviewed work reports FS22 emphasizes yield/expansion and largely overlooks environmental feedback loops (players cannot meaningfully damage soil/biodiversity through heavy machinery/agrochemical overuse in core framing); some mods add penalties. — [source: https://www.gu.se/en/news/in-the-farmers-video-game-expansion-trumps-sustainability (unrecognized host, institutional summary linking *Journal of Rural Studies* article https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0743016725001263)] (confidence: **high** for reported finding; page-level details **medium** without direct article read in this pass)

## Evidence Against the Idea

- **Opacity/punishment risk in cozy context:** hidden or poorly telegraphed long-term soil penalties can feel unfair and conflict with cozy expectations (safety/abundance/softness; “recompensar, nunca pregar”). UX/design literature and community sentiment indicate feedback clarity and recoverability are critical. A Joja/agrochemical penalty that is invisible, irreversible, or hard to understand may frustrate players. — [source: https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/a-quick-ux-lesson-from-gdc-masterclass-teacher-celia-hodent ; https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/feedback-in-games-how-to-design-rewards-and-punishments ; repo GDD pillars/Horseshoe notes; community examples cited by subagent (Steam/Gamerant/ModDrop reviews context, **secondary, medium**)] (confidence: **medium–high** as a risk; **ASSUMPTION** that Living Roots audience shares typical cozy sensitivities)
- **Balance/degenerate strategy risk:** short-term gain + long-term cost can create spreadsheet micromanagement or a dominant “use chemical then repair” loop (RF4 optimization behavior illustrates this class of risk) unless magnitudes, stacking, visibility, and recovery are tuned and testable. — [source: RF4 community optimization threads (secondary); FS22 modded penalties as contrast — https://www.gu.se/en/news/in-the-farmers-video-game-expansion-trumps-sustainability ] (confidence: **medium**)
- **Complexity/MP/test cost:** fertilizer application touches item use, terrain mutation, Harmony patching, and multiplayer authority — easy to introduce desync/duplication/edge cases (probe paths, UI error paths, farmhand apply, save/load). D1 live attestation is currently blocked, so MP confidence may remain `machine-pass`/partial. — [source: SMAPI/1.6 modding constraints; subagent Harmony/MP analysis; specs 014/015 blockers] (confidence: **high** for risk presence)
- **Weak external evidence of direct demand for this exact mechanic in SDV mods:** accessible search did not find a verified “chemical fertilizer degrades soil” SV mod; closest clear prior art found was Joja-vs-organic *benefit/access* tradeoff (*More Fertilizers*), not degradation. This could mean niche/unproven demand **or** access/search limitations. — [source: prior-art subagent; Nexus 403] (confidence: **medium**)
- **Narrative/tone risk:** framing “Joja” content can be compelling if it reinforces the agroecology thesis, but can also read as preachy if the penalty feels punitive rather than instructive — directly relevant to GDD “recompensar, nunca pregar”. — [source: `docs/GDD-living-roots.md`; **ASSUMPTION**]
